# Cycle and module benchmarks

Research date: 2026-11-19. This is a source comparison, not a claim that every feature is missing in Weft. Plane's local checkout is `/home/plong/workspace/plane`, revision `efa46d2840`; it and current hosted documentation may differ by release and edition. No product was exercised in a browser for this note.

## High-value workflows to compare

1. **Reliable cycle review and transfer.** Plane transfers backlog, unstarted and started work from a completed cycle to an active, upcoming or draft cycle; completed and cancelled work remain. Its documentation says the transfer is offered once per cycle. [Plane cycles](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/cycles). The local UI uses a completed cycle's `progress_snapshot` where present ([sidebar-details.tsx](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/web/components/cycles/analytics-sidebar/sidebar-details.tsx#L44)). This suggests separately checking whether Weft preserves original scope and outcomes when moving items: transfer convenience alone is not historical reporting.
2. **Progress that explains changes.** Plane's cycle details break down work by state, assignee and label and chart work items or estimates; its active cycle build-up option is documented as Pro. The local chart switches item/point data ([sidebar-chart.tsx](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/web/components/cycles/analytics-sidebar/sidebar-chart.tsx#L38)). [Plane cycles](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/cycles). Jira's sprint burndown includes a scope-change log and distinct completed, incomplete, removed and completed-outside-sprint lists. [Jira sprint burndown](https://support.atlassian.com/jira-software-cloud/docs/what-is-the-sprint-burndown-report/). Linear's cycle graph separates scope, started and completed work and target. [Linear cycles](https://linear.app/docs/use-cycles). These are benchmarks for reviewability, not evidence that a current-scope burndown is equivalent to a historical scope report.
3. **Module risk and context.** Plane shows state-group counts, blocked and overdue counts, breakdown by state/assignee/label/priority, clickable breakdown filters and links on a module. Its local sidebar renders module links ([root.tsx](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/web/components/modules/analytics-sidebar/root.tsx#L420)). [Plane modules](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/modules). Linear project updates add a human health signal and narrative about challenges and next steps; its projects are a related but broader construct than Plane modules. [Linear updates](https://linear.app/docs/initiative-and-project-updates).
4. **Planning across modules.** Plane provides list, gallery and draggable date timeline, plus search, sorts, status/lead/member/date filters and favorites; local layout icons include list, board and gantt ([module-layout-icon.tsx](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/web/components/modules/module-layout-icon.tsx#L23)). [Plane modules](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/modules). Linear milestones add dated checkpoints with issue progress within a project, but are not the same object as a module. [Linear milestones](https://linear.app/docs/project-milestones).

## Additional design choices

- **Cycle cadence and drafting:** Plane permits unscheduled draft cycles, auto-scheduling and, in Enterprise Grid, parallel cycles. [Plane cycles](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/cycles). Linear generates recurring team cycles with a configured cadence and optional cooldown and automatically rolls unfinished work into the next cycle. [Linear cycles](https://linear.app/docs/use-cycles). Automatic rollover changes product semantics, so it should be an explicit design decision, not assumed parity.
- **Capacity and velocity:** Linear estimates cycle capacity from the previous three completed cycles (or initially from team size). Its cycle graph displays scope and throughput; its project graph also predicts completion from historical velocity and shows optimistic/pessimistic ranges when sufficient data exists. [Linear cycles](https://linear.app/docs/use-cycles), [Linear project graph](https://linear.app/docs/project-graph). These need trustworthy historical data first.
- **Discovery and export:** Plane indexes cycle and module names in workspace search; filtered exports from a specific cycle or module are documented as Pro. [Plane search](https://docs.plane.so/workspaces-and-users/search-workspace), [Plane export](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/export).
- **Semantics:** Plane modules group work by goal and are project-scoped, with manual state, lead and members; one work item may be in several modules. Cycle dates normally cannot overlap unless parallel cycles are enabled; one work item may be in only one cycle. [Plane modules](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/modules), [Plane cycles](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/cycles). Compare definitions before treating differences as defects.

## Weft gap audit

Weft inspected at `ba6539c`, by reading source and existing tests; no runtime or browser validation was performed. Priority reflects the impact on a small software team, not a validated customer ranking.

**Descriptions:** Plane's Cycle and Module each have a plain-text description field, exposed in their create/edit forms and shown on their detail sidebars ([Cycle model](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/api/plane/db/models/cycle.py#L62), [Cycle form](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/web/components/cycles/form.tsx#L145), [Cycle sidebar](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/web/components/cycles/analytics-sidebar/sidebar-details.tsx#L73), [Module model](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/api/plane/db/models/module.py#L69), [Module form](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/web/components/modules/form.tsx#L159), [Module sidebar](/home/plong/workspace/plane/apps/web/components/modules/analytics-sidebar/root.tsx#L298)). The Module model also declares rich-text fields, but their use is not established by this comparison. Weft's Cycle and Module Actions and forms have no entity description field; empty-state descriptions are unrelated. Add optional descriptions to both if parity is desired, including shared Action schemas and all clients.

Weft already has project-scoped Cycles with date-derived status, overlap warnings, progress, state breakdown, item/point burndown, manual transfer, archive and workspace active-cycle list. Modules have status, lead, members, optional dates, multiple memberships, progress, item/point burndown, list/grid and archive. Both expose shared Actions to other clients. See [cycles.ts](../../packages/actions/src/cycles.ts), [modules.ts](../../packages/actions/src/modules.ts), [Cycle UI](../../apps/web/src/cycles.tsx), [Module UI](../../apps/web/src/modules.tsx), and [MCP actions](../mcp.md).

1. **P0 — Cycle history and trustworthy charts.** Transfer updates the current membership row, and progress and charts query current members. After transferring unfinished items, they disappear from the original cycle's totals. The burndown applies current membership, current points, current state and the current completion timestamp to every past day; scope changes, later cancellations, changes in estimates, and reopening can rewrite history. Modules use the same calculation. Example: move two unfinished items from a past cycle containing four items with two complete: its current progress becomes 2/2, rather than preserving 2/4 at close. Preserve historical membership and changes, or a clearly defined close snapshot plus sufficient event history; specify treatment of reopened items, added/removed scope and point changes. A snapshot alone does not reconstruct a daily scope-change chart. [Cycle counts, chart and transfer](../../packages/actions/src/cycles.ts), [Module chart](../../packages/actions/src/modules.ts), [membership schema](../../packages/db/migrations/0022_cycles.sql).
2. **P0 — Archived Cycle immutability.** The glossary says archived entities cannot be edited, but `update_cycle`, `remove_cycle_items`, and transfer from an archived completed cycle have no archive check; the detail UI also offers edit and remove to writers. The Module actions explicitly check archive state for these kinds of writes. Align shared Action guards and UI, and test direct Action calls as well as the interface. [Glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md), [Cycle Actions](../../packages/actions/src/cycles.ts), [Cycle detail](../../apps/web/src/cycles.tsx), [Module Actions](../../packages/actions/src/modules.ts).
3. **P1 — Cycle planning and closeout.** The Cycle add dialog lists candidates one by one without search or multi-select, even though the Action supports up to 500; items in another cycle are only reported skipped. Transfer moves all unfinished work to one existing active/upcoming cycle, with no per-item choice or preview. Add searchable multi-select, show current cycle and explicit move choices, and offer a review of what will move and what stays. Keep Weft's human-directed transfer unless the product deliberately changes it. [Cycle UI](../../apps/web/src/cycles.tsx), [Cycle Actions](../../packages/actions/src/cycles.ts).
4. **P1 — Module context and risk.** The Module model has name, status, people and dates but no description, links, health update, or explicit blocked/overdue summary. Its page shows work items and burndown; it does not provide Plane's module-level risk breakdown and drill-down. Start with description/links and counts for blocked and overdue work, with clear definitions and clickable filtered lists; assess narrative health updates separately. [Module schema](../../packages/db/migrations/0025_modules.sql), [Module UI](../../apps/web/src/modules.tsx), [Plane modules](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/modules).
5. **P1 — Compare and find Modules.** Weft's Module index has list/grid but no search, status/lead/member/date filters, sorting or timeline of Modules. Work item layouts include a Gantt, which is distinct from a cross-Module timeline. Add discovery controls first and a Module timeline when teams need date coordination. [Module index](../../apps/web/src/modules.tsx), [work item layouts](../../apps/web/src/work-item-layouts.tsx), [Plane modules](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/modules).
6. **P2 — Reporting, cadence and capacity.** Weft has state breakdown and basic burndown, but not the cited Plane assignee/label breakdown or Jira scope-change report. Cycle creation requires dates; there is no draft or recurring scheduling in its Action. Evaluate optional cadence, capacity and velocity only after historical reporting is reliable. Automatic rollover is a product decision because the glossary explicitly requires someone to transfer unfinished work. [Cycle Actions](../../packages/actions/src/cycles.ts), [Glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md), [Plane cycles](https://docs.plane.so/core-concepts/cycles), [Linear cycles](https://linear.app/docs/use-cycles), [Jira burndown](https://support.atlassian.com/jira-software-cloud/docs/what-is-the-sprint-burndown-report/).

Further validation: exercise close/transfer/archive/reopen with representative data, decide what a historical report promises, and test planning with a team. Edition-specific Plane features and broader Linear project/milestone concepts are examples, not parity requirements.
